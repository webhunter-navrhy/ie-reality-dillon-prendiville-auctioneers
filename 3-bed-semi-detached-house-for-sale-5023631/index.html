About the property

Description property

39 Bruach na Gaile, Moyvane presents a wonderful opportunity to acquire a beautifully situated 3-bedroom semi-detached residence within a mature completed cul-de-sac development. Perfectly blending village charm with modern convenience, the property is within effortless walking distance of all local village amenities, including the local primary school, shops, church, welcoming traditional pubs, tranquil woods and a nature trail way. Inside, the C rated home offers a cozy atmosphere anchored by an open fireplace and efficient oil-fired central heating, alongside the comfort of double-glazed uPVC windows throughout.

High-speed fibre broadband availability makes it ideal for remote work, while seamless connections to all essential public services ensure complete peace of mind. Accommodation comprises entrance hallway and downstairs wc, kitchen, sitting room, 3 bedrooms (master ensuite) and family bathroom.

Externally, the property boasts a secure gated entrance paired with a low-maintenance concrete driveway that provides convenient off-street parking along with manicured lawns front and rear. Its location is exceptionally well-connected—just a 10-minute drive from the historic heritage town of Listowel and approximately 25 minutes from Ballybunion’s stunning Blue Flag beaches and world-renowned championship golf courses. Whether you are a first-time buyer, looking for a peaceful holiday retreat, downsizing for retirement, or seeking a permanent family residence, this property offers the perfect canvas for your next chapter.
